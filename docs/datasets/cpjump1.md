# CPJUMP1 (cpg0000-jump-pilot) dossier

A primary-source rundown of `cpg0000-jump-pilot`, the JUMP-Cell Painting Consortium pilot dataset (CPJUMP1). This dossier exists because CPJUMP1 plays a specific narrow role in cellduet's v0 design: it is the **same-cell-line A549 sanity-check arm** of a three-arm cross-modal phenotype concordance study, complementary to the U2OS-only `cpg0016-jump` primary arm characterised in [`jump-cp-cpg0016.md`](./jump-cp-cpg0016.md). Numbers below were re-verified against the public AWS S3 bucket `s3://cellpainting-gallery/cpg0000-jump-pilot/`, the consortium's own repository [`jump-cellpainting/2024_Chandrasekaran_NatureMethods`](https://github.com/jump-cellpainting/2024_Chandrasekaran_NatureMethods), and the [`jump-cellpainting/JUMP-Target`](https://github.com/jump-cellpainting/JUMP-Target) plate-map repository directly during research. The Tahoe ∩ CPJUMP1 InChIKey overlap was computed locally on the actual TSV against Tahoe-100M's `drug_metadata.parquet`.

## Origin and citation

CPJUMP1 was generated by the **JUMP-Cell Painting Consortium** (Broad Institute coordination; same membership as `cpg0016`) in the **2020-11-04 to 2020-12-08** window of imaging batches, as the methodological pilot study that benchmarked the assay choices later locked in for the production `cpg0016-jump` release. Lead authors are **Srinivas Niranj Chandrasekaran, Beth A. Cimini, Anne E. Carpenter, and Shantanu Singh**.

**Dataset paper.** Chandrasekaran et al., *"Three million images and morphological profiles of cells treated with matched chemical and genetic perturbations,"* **Nature Methods** **21**, 1114–1121 (2024), DOI [10.1038/s41592-024-02241-6](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-024-02241-6), open access at [PMC11166567](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11166567/). The earlier preprint version is bioRxiv [2022.01.05.475090](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2022.01.05.475090v3) (v3, May 2024). Both bioRxiv and PMC returned 403 / 303 to direct fetches during research; version-specific deltas were not pulled. The companion repository [`jump-cellpainting/2024_Chandrasekaran_NatureMethods`](https://github.com/jump-cellpainting/2024_Chandrasekaran_NatureMethods) ships the canonical experiment-metadata table, plate maps, CellProfiler pipelines, DeepProfiler config, and benchmark notebooks.

**Release date.** Imaging batches Nov–Dec 2020; preprint posted Jan 2022 (v1) with v3 in May 2024; Nature Methods publication May 2024; the `cpg0000-jump-pilot` S3 prefix has files dated 2023-02-09/10 with a 2026-02-09 update to the harmonised metadata CSV.

**License.** **Dual.** Source code in the analysis repository is **BSD 3-Clause** ([LICENSE_BSD3.md](https://github.com/jump-cellpainting/2024_Chandrasekaran_NatureMethods/blob/main/LICENSE_BSD3.md)); the **data, results, and figures are CC0 1.0** ([LICENSE_CC0.md](https://github.com/jump-cellpainting/2024_Chandrasekaran_NatureMethods/blob/main/LICENSE_CC0.md)). The Cell Painting Gallery itself is end-to-end CC0 ([gallery README](https://github.com/broadinstitute/cellpainting-gallery), [AWS Open Data registry](https://registry.opendata.aws/cellpainting-gallery/)). The CPJUMP1 well-level profiles, deep features, raw images, and metadata that cellduet consumes are all CC0.

**Canonical hosting.** AWS S3 `s3://cellpainting-gallery/cpg0000-jump-pilot/`, public no-sign-request, served via the AWS Open Data Program. The pilot lives entirely under a single source prefix, **`source_4`** (the Broad Institute's own data-generating source); no other source contributed to CPJUMP1. The bucket layout is `cpg0000-jump-pilot/source_4/{images,workspace}/`. The whole pilot occupies the same gallery-wide CC0 license stack as the production set.

## Biological scope

CPJUMP1 is **a comparative study of perturbation modalities** in two cell lines, where the same target gene set was perturbed three different ways (compound, ORF over-expression, CRISPR-Cas9 knockout) so that the consortium could measure cross-modality concordance, replicate-retrieval performance, time-course stability, and assay parameters before scaling up to the 116k-compound `cpg0016` release.

**Perturbation types and counts.** The pilot used the **JUMP-Target-1 plate set** ([`jump-cellpainting/JUMP-Target`](https://github.com/jump-cellpainting/JUMP-Target)):

- **JUMP-Target-1-Compound: 306 unique broad_sample IDs / 302 unique InChIKeys / 303 unique pert_iname** on a single 384-well layout (verified locally on `JUMP-Target-1_compound_metadata.tsv`: 307 rows minus header = 306 entries; the 1-row "303 vs 302" delta is the quinine / quinidine stereoisomer pair plus a duplicate broad_sample). Of the 306, **260 are `pert_type == trt` (treatments) and 47 are `control`** (positive controls of three flavours: `poscon_orf` 6, `poscon_cp` 26, `poscon_diverse` 14, plus DMSO `negcon`). Recommended dose: **5 µM** (the JUMP-Target README; the production `cpg0016` later switched to a different dose schedule).
- **JUMP-Target-1-CRISPR: 335 sgRNAs targeting 175 genes**, 88 of which are control sgRNAs (5 `poscon_orf`, 25 `poscon_cp`, 28 `poscon_diverse`, 30 non-targeting / intergenic `negcon`). Production cells carry Cas9.
- **JUMP-Target-1-ORF: 175 ORFs targeting 175 genes**, 45 of which are controls.

The compound, ORF, and CRISPR plates share **160 genes in common** (the compound targets that also have matching ORF + sgRNA reagents), which is the pilot's whole point: gene-vs-compound replicate-retrieval AP scores can be computed across modalities on these 160 genes. The "302 curated compounds" / "303 compounds" / "306 broad_sample" figures all refer to the same compound set with different counting rules; the dossier uses **302 unique InChIKeys** as the canonical compound count.

**Cell lines profiled.** **A549 + U2OS, both human cancer lines.** A549 is human lung adenocarcinoma (CVCL_0023, ATCC CCL-185, KRAS G12S, TP53 wild-type per DepMap); U2OS is human osteosarcoma (CVCL_0042, ATCC HTB-96, P53-mutant near-tetraploid). Both lines are profiled in `Parental` form, and A549 additionally has a `Cas9` variant (the same line stably expressing Cas9, used for the CRISPR arm and a small compound-in-Cas9-line cross-check). U2OS in CPJUMP1 is profiled `Parental`-only for the compound arm. **A549 is in Tahoe-100M's 50-cancer-line panel**; U2OS is not. This is the cell-line-context fact that makes CPJUMP1 the same-cell-line sanity-check arm.

**Cell Painting protocol.** Five-channel fluorescence per the [Bray 2016 *Nat Protoc*](https://doi.org/10.1038/nprot.2016.105) optimised assay (refined further in [Cimini 2023 *Nat Protoc*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41596-023-00840-9)): **DNA (Hoechst 33342), RNA / nucleoli (SYTO14), ER (concanavalin A), AGP (phalloidin + WGA), mitochondria (MitoTracker)**, captured at **20× water-immersion** on a PerkinElmer Opera Phenix. Per the consortium README, **8 images per site (5 fluorescent channels + 3 brightfield z-planes)**, and **typically 9 sites per well** (ranging 7–16 depending on plate). The protocol is the same one `cpg0016` later used; the pilot was the protocol-optimisation study.

**Plate, well, batch, image structure (verified empirically against S3 listings).**

- **Six imaging batches** under `cpg0000-jump-pilot/source_4/workspace/profiles/`: `2020_11_04_CPJUMP1` (the main 51-plate batch), `2020_11_04_CPJUMP1_DL` (10-plate DeepLearning batch with extra fields), `2020_11_18_CPJUMP1_TimepointDay1`, `2020_11_19_TimepointDay4`, `2020_12_02_CPJUMP1_2WeeksTimePoint`, `2020_12_07_CPJUMP1_4WeeksTimePoint`, `2020_12_08_CPJUMP1_Bleaching` (a photobleaching QC sub-experiment that re-images the same compound plates 2–7 times each).
- **117 plate-batches total** across all six batches (verified: line count of `experiment-metadata.tsv` is 118 = 1 header + 117 plates). Many of these are repeats of the same physical plate at different timepoints or with different staining conditions.
- **52 compound plates** (40 in A549, 12 in U2OS), **22 CRISPR plates** (10 A549, 12 U2OS), **43 ORF plates** (21 A549, 22 U2OS) (verified by groupby on `experiment-metadata.tsv`).
- **384 wells per plate** (standard JUMP layout).
- **~3 million images total** (the paper title figure; consistent with 117 plates × ~9 sites × 384 wells × 8 channels ≈ 3.2M).
- **Replicate structure.** On each compound plate the JUMP-Target-1 layout has n=2 replicate wells for each of 14 `poscon_diverse` compounds, n=64 DMSO wells, and **n=1 well for each of the remaining ~245 compounds**. Cross-plate replication (the same plate map run multiple times in different physical plates) is what produces the higher per-(compound, cell-line, timepoint) replicate count, see the A549 section below.

## A549-specific scope

This is the section that determines CPJUMP1's value to cellduet. Plate-level facts verified by groupby on the consortium's [`benchmark/output/experiment-metadata.tsv`](https://github.com/jump-cellpainting/2024_Chandrasekaran_NatureMethods/blob/main/benchmark/output/experiment-metadata.tsv).

**Compound plates in A549 (40 plate-batches total, but most are repeats):**

| Batch | Plates | Time | Density | Cell line | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `2020_11_04_CPJUMP1` | BR00116991–94 (4 plates) | 24 h | 100% | Parental | WGA staining anomaly flagged |
| `2020_11_04_CPJUMP1` | BR00117008–09 (2 plates) | 48 h | 80% | Parental | low density |
| `2020_11_04_CPJUMP1` | BR00117015–17, BR00117019 (4 plates) | 48 h | 100% | Parental | the "canonical" A549 condition |
| `2020_11_04_CPJUMP1` | BR00117054–55 (2 plates) | 48 h | 120% | Parental | high density |
| `2020_11_04_CPJUMP1` | BR00117050–53 (4 plates) | 48 h | 100% | Cas9 (A549-Cas9 line) | compound-in-Cas9-cells cross-check |
| `2020_12_08_CPJUMP1_Bleaching` | BR00116991A–F, ...994A–F (24 plates) | 24 h | 100% | Parental | photobleaching QC, 6 re-images of the 4 main 24-h plates |

**The cellduet operating slice.** Excluding the bleaching QC re-imaging (which is the same physical plates re-imaged 2–7 times for a photobleaching-stability experiment, not biological replicates) and excluding the Cas9 compound cross-check (different cell sub-line), the **biological-replicate-bearing A549 compound slice is 12 plates** in the `2020_11_04_CPJUMP1` batch: 4 × 24h-100%-density + 2 × 48h-80% + 4 × 48h-100% + 2 × 48h-120%. If the analysis fixes density at 100% (the standard condition), the slice tightens to **8 plates: 4 × 24h + 4 × 48h**, both in A549-Parental. If the analysis fixes both density (100%) and a single timepoint, the slice tightens to **4 plates per (timepoint, density) cell**.

**Replicate count per compound in A549-Parental at the canonical 100%-density condition.** Each of the 4 plates per (timepoint, 100% density) condition is an independent 384-well JUMP-Target-1 layout. For the **245 once-per-plate compounds**, that gives **n=4 replicate wells per (compound, timepoint) at 100% density**, or **n=8 if both timepoints are pooled**. For the **14 `poscon_diverse` compounds with 2 wells/plate**, that gives n=8 per (timepoint), or n=16 pooled. Adding the density-perturbation plates and the Cas9 compound plates back in roughly doubles the total well count, but those wells are in different experimental conditions so they don't increase the apparent-replicate count of a fixed-condition analysis.

**Total A549 compound well count (canonical condition).** 4 plates × 384 wells = **1,536 wells per (timepoint)**, **3,072 wells across both 24 h + 48 h**. Of those, 14 wells per plate are `poscon_diverse` (2 wells × 7 unique compounds in the layout), 64 wells per plate are DMSO, and the remaining **~245 wells per plate are unique compound treatments**.

**A549 compound count.** All compound A549 plates use the **same JUMP-Target-1_compound_platemap**, so every A549 compound plate contains the same **302 unique compounds** (plus DMSO). There is no compound subsetting per cell line; the JUMP-Target-1 layout is one fixed plate map. **All 302 CPJUMP1 compounds are profiled in A549.** This matters because the Tahoe ∩ CPJUMP1 InChIKey overlap (next sections) is therefore identical to Tahoe ∩ A549.

## Pre-computed artifacts

Empirical inventory from the S3 bucket, May 2026:

**(a) CellProfiler hand-engineered features.** Six processing stages per plate (raw aggregated, augmented, normalised whole-plate, normalised negcon, feature-selected whole-plate, feature-selected negcon, spherised), produced by **pycytominer** following the [recipe in `2024_Chandrasekaran_NatureMethods/run.sh`](https://github.com/jump-cellpainting/2024_Chandrasekaran_NatureMethods/blob/main/run.sh). Feature dimensionality at each stage:

- Raw aggregated: ~**4,762 CellProfiler features per well** (consistent with the cpg0016 plate parquets; same pipeline).
- Feature-selected: low-hundreds of features (1.08 MB / plate at gzip ratio implies on the order of 100–300 surviving features depending on plate).
- Spherised: full feature vector after a whitening transform applied across the whole batch.

License: CC0-1.0 (data layer of the dual-license repo; gallery-wide CC0).

**(b) DeepProfiler EfficientNet features.** Available for **10 plates** under `workspace/profiles/2020_11_04_CPJUMP1_DL/` (verified empirically: BR00116996–999, BR00117010–013, BR00118039–040). These are well-aggregated profiles produced by [DeepProfiler](https://github.com/cytomining/DeepProfiler) running an EfficientNet-B0 trained on Cell Painting imagery, then aggregated to well-level by mean of single-cell embeddings (the consortium's `deep_profiles/README.md` is the source). Plate-file sizes: `<plate>.csv.gz` ≈ **22 MB**, `_augmented.csv.gz` ≈ 7 MB, `_spherized.csv.gz` ≈ 32 MB. Exact embedding dimensionality is not stated on the plate file size alone; per the DeepProfiler default config and the cpg0016 cpcnn parallel, **expect a 672-dimensional embedding per well** (the same EfficientNet-B0 architecture cpg0016 uses). License: CC0 on the data; the underlying DeepProfiler model is BSD-3-Clause.

**Coverage gap.** The 10 DL plates are a **subset** of the 117-plate study, weighted toward the main `2020_11_04_CPJUMP1` compound and CRISPR plates. Cross-checking against the experiment metadata, the 10 DL plates include both A549 and U2OS compound plates and both compound and CRISPR perturbation types, but **not all A549 compound plates have a DL profile**. For the v0 A549 compound slice the DL feature is available for at most a few plates, not all 16; the canonical CellProfiler profile is the safer primary feature.

**(c) cpcnn (CellPainting-CNN v1) deep features.** **Not present** under `cpg0000-jump-pilot/` as of May 2026. The cpcnn workspace_dl tree exists for `cpg0016-jump` but **not for the pilot**; the pilot's deep-learning offering is the older DeepProfiler EfficientNet-B0 set described above. If the cellduet plan wants the cpcnn 672-d embedding for direct head-to-head with `cpg0016`, the DeepProfiler EfficientNet output is the closest analog, not an identical artifact.

**(d) EfficientNet-V2-S ImageNet-21k embeddings.** **Not present** under `cpg0000-jump-pilot/`. This artifact is `cpg0016`-only.

**(e) cpdistiller_mesmer / OpenPhenom / Phenom-1 / Phenom-2 embeddings.** **Not present** under `cpg0000-jump-pilot/`. Same gap as on `cpg0016`.

**Recommended v0 artifact: `<plate>_normalized_feature_select_negcon_batch.csv.gz`** for each of the 4–16 A549 compound plates of interest. Reasons: feature-selected (small file, ~1 MB / plate), DMSO-control normalised (the right reference for compound-vs-vehicle phenotype), batch-aware (the only batch is `2020_11_04_CPJUMP1` so the batch term is a no-op but the file is the same shape as the cpg0016 equivalents), and CC0. The DeepProfiler EfficientNet embedding is the secondary head-to-head artifact for a sensitivity analysis if cpcnn-style deep features are wanted, available for the 10 DL-enabled plates.

## Compute footprint for v0

Colab Free is the binding constraint: **~12 GB RAM, 15 GB persistent Drive, T4 with ~15 GB VRAM, 12-hour session, ~90-min idle disconnect**.

**Smallest useful slice for cellduet.** The 14-compound Tahoe-overlap intersection in A549-Parental at 100% density:

1. Pull **8 per-plate `_normalized_feature_select_negcon_batch.csv.gz` files** (4 × 24h + 4 × 48h) over HTTPS from S3. **8 plates × 1.08 MB ≈ 9 MB** download. Trivial.
2. Concatenate to **8 plates × 384 wells = 3,072 well rows × ~150 features**, filter to the **14 Tahoe-overlap InChIKeys** by joining against `JUMP-Target-1_compound_metadata.tsv` (40 KB). Output: **14 compounds × ~4 wells per (compound, timepoint) × 2 timepoints ≈ 112 well rows × ~150 features × float32 ≈ 70 KB**. The 14 `poscon_diverse` matches contribute proportionally more wells. Trivially in RAM.
3. Aggregate per-(compound, timepoint) by median across replicate wells, then either pool timepoints or keep them separate. Output: **14 compounds × ~150 features × float32 ≈ 8 KB** pooled, or **14 × 2 × ~150 × float32 ≈ 17 KB** stratified.
4. Compute pairwise cosine distance: **14 × 14 floats ≈ 800 bytes**. Microseconds.
5. Mirror against the Tahoe-100M A549 transcriptomic phenotype (per [`tahoe-100m.md`](./tahoe-100m.md), pseudobulk LFC across HVGs filtered to A549) restricted to the same 14 compounds. **14 × ~2,000 HVGs × float32 ≈ 110 KB**. Compute the cross-modal Mantel correlation on the 14×14 distance matrices.

**What does not fit.** Nothing about CPJUMP1 stresses Colab Free. The whole 117-plate × ~58-MB CSV bundle is ~6.8 GB; the metadata_harmonized CSV is 2.2 GB; both fit on Drive with margin. The raw image corpus (3M × ~5 MB TIFFs = ~15 TB) is the only thing that would not fit, and v0 does not touch raw imagery.

**Persistent caches.** The per-(compound, feature) A549 aggregate (~10 KB) is small enough to ship as part of the cellduet HF dataset alongside the cpg0016 aggregate. The per-well filtered slice (~70 KB) is tiny enough to recompute every session without caching.

## Aggregation considerations

CPJUMP1's pre-built profile pipeline already does the well-level CellProfiler aggregation (per-cell → per-site → per-well, mean) and the per-plate normalisation (MAD-robustised to whole-plate or to DMSO `negcon` wells). The v0 starting point is therefore well-level, not single-cell. Two further aggregation steps remain:

1. **Replicate-well aggregation per (compound, plate, timepoint).** For each compound on each plate, collapse the n=1 (or n=2 for `poscon_diverse`) wells to a single feature vector. With n=1 this is a no-op; with n=2 it is a median.
2. **Cross-plate aggregation per (compound, timepoint).** Average the per-plate vectors across the 4 replicate plates per (timepoint, 100% density). This is the n=4 step that gives statistical stability.

**Recommendation: use `pycytominer.consensus(method='median')`** the same way the production `cpg0016` recipe does. The CPJUMP1 paper repo uses the same pycytominer API throughout; the consortium's own benchmark notebooks ([`benchmark/1.calculate-map-cp.ipynb`](https://github.com/jump-cellpainting/2024_Chandrasekaran_NatureMethods/blob/main/benchmark/1.calculate-map-cp.ipynb)) consume the `_normalized_feature_select_negcon_batch.csv.gz` files directly, then call `mean_average_precision` per perturbation. The cellduet pipeline can mirror that exactly for the 14-compound subset.

**Difference vs `cpg0016` recipe.** Three:

- CPJUMP1 ships **CSV.GZ**, `cpg0016-jump-assembled` ships **parquet**. The cellduet reader needs both code paths.
- CPJUMP1's feature selection is **per-plate** (file suffix `_feature_select_batch` / `_feature_select_negcon_batch`), so the feature schema can differ across plates. Joining plates requires either intersecting feature sets or recomputing feature selection on the concatenated frame. `cpg0016` ships a single 737-feature harmony parquet with one fixed schema; CPJUMP1 does not.
- CPJUMP1 has **no Harmony batch correction** in the consortium pipeline (the [profiling-recipe](https://github.com/broadinstitute/jump-profiling-recipe) Harmony step was added for the cross-source `cpg0016` assembly; CPJUMP1 is single-source so cross-source Harmony is not applicable). Plate-level batch effects within CPJUMP1 are addressed by the per-plate MAD normalisation, not by Harmony.

## Known caveats

- **Small N for the v0 sanity-check.** The Tahoe ∩ CPJUMP1 InChIKey overlap is **14 compounds** (verified, see next section), not the 228 of the cpg0016 primary arm. A 14-point Mantel correlation on 14×14 distance matrices has **91 pairs** and weak per-cell statistical power. The cellduet writeup must frame this arm as **qualitative** confirmation of pattern direction, not as an independent significance test. A flat-shaped scatter of "14 morphological distances vs 14 transcriptomic distances on the same drug pairs in the same cell line" is the right deliverable, plus a Spearman ρ and 95 % bootstrap CI; a p-value at N=14 will rarely be informative.
- **Perturbation-type confound.** CPJUMP1 mixes compound + ORF + CRISPR plates in the same study. The cellduet A549 sanity-check must filter to **`Perturbation == 'compound'` plates only** and **`pert_type == 'trt'`** wells (or include controls explicitly with their `control_type` annotation). Failing to filter pulls CRISPR / ORF wells into the morphological feature aggregate and breaks the comparison.
- **Three of the 14 matches are control compounds.** Two `poscon_diverse` (Lonafarnib, Quinidine via skeleton match) and one `poscon_cp` (Filgotinib). The `poscon_diverse` set has **n=2 wells/plate** instead of n=1, so those three compounds have ~2× the replicate density of the other 11 in the joint set. The cellduet aggregation should treat all 14 uniformly (median across all available wells), but the writeup should note that the joint-set is 11 trt + 3 control for transparency.
- **Plate / batch artifacts and density confound.** The 12 A549 compound plates in `2020_11_04_CPJUMP1` span three densities (80%, 100%, 120%) and two timepoints (24 h, 48 h). The "WGA staining anomaly" annotation on the 4 × 24-h plates flags a known assay-level artifact that may bias those plates' AGP channel features. **Recommendation: pin the v0 sanity check to 100% density only** and report 24 h and 48 h separately to avoid confounding density and timepoint into the modality comparison.
- **Pilot's intentionally small scope.** CPJUMP1 was a **methods optimisation** study, not a discovery atlas. Its 302-compound layout was deliberately small so the consortium could vary timepoint, density, antibiotic, Cas9 status, and bleaching schedules in a fully crossed design. The biology covered is the JUMP-Target compound set's intentionally diverse target space (160 genes across kinases, GPCRs, hormone receptors, epigenetic regulators, metabolic enzymes), not a pharmacologically deep coverage of any single MoA. A 14-compound Tahoe overlap is therefore not unrepresentative of the underlying biology; it is a thin sample of a deliberately diverse target panel.
- **CSV.GZ not parquet.** Reading 16 plates × ~1 MB CSV.GZ via pandas is fast but slower than the parquet equivalent; the cellduet code path needs a small wrapper that handles both formats.
- **DeepProfiler embeddings cover only 10 of 117 plates.** A direct head-to-head between CellProfiler and deep features in the A549 sanity-check arm is bounded by which of the 4 canonical 100%-density plates have a DL profile; expect 1–4 plates of overlap, n bounded accordingly.
- **No JUMP-Target-2 compatibility shim.** CPJUMP1 uses **JUMP-Target-1**; `cpg0016` production uses **JUMP-Target-2**. The compound sets are identical at the InChIKey level (the `JUMP-Target/README.md` confirms this) but `broad_sample` IDs differ. Joining CPJUMP1 to `cpg0016` requires keying on InChIKey, not on `broad_sample`.

## Joint-with-Tahoe-A549 considerations

**Cell-line context: A549 is matched.** A549 / CVCL_0023 / ACH-000681 is in **both** Tahoe-100M's 50-cancer-line panel and CPJUMP1's two-cell-line panel. Both datasets profile *human lung adenocarcinoma, KRAS-G12S, TP53 wild-type*. This is **the cleanest possible same-cell same-perturbation cross-modal match in the entire cellduet design**. The cpg0016 primary arm compares 47 cancer cell lines (Tahoe) vs U2OS (cpg0016); the rxrx3 robustness arm compares 47 cancer cell lines vs HUVEC; only the CPJUMP1 sanity-check arm has a literal cell-line match.

**Tahoe A549 transcriptomic depth.** Per [`tahoe-100m.md`](./tahoe-100m.md), Tahoe's 50-line panel is dominated by lung (27 lines) with A549 as one of the two most-cited lines. The pseudobulk-DE table covers all (drug, A549, plate) combinations for all 379 Tahoe drugs; the per-(drug, A549) demuxed cell count is bounded by the cell-village demux performance and is open question 3 in `tahoe-100m.md` (anything below ~50 cells per group is too noisy for stable LFC). For the 14 Tahoe ∩ CPJUMP1 drugs in A549, expect ~1 well per plate × ~3 doses × however many plates each drug appears on; verify in notebook 01.

**Joint-set protocol for the sanity-check arm.**

1. From CPJUMP1: 8 plates of `_normalized_feature_select_negcon_batch.csv.gz` (4 × 24h + 4 × 48h, 100% density, A549-Parental, compound), filter to the 14 InChIKeys, median-aggregate to per-(compound, timepoint).
2. From Tahoe-100M: pseudobulk LFC restricted to A549-only cells, per (drug, dose, A549), aggregated to per-drug across doses, restricted to the same 14 drugs.
3. Both modalities → 14 × feature-vector matrix → 14 × 14 cosine distance matrix → Mantel correlation, scatter, Spearman ρ, bootstrap CI.
4. Compare against the cpg0016 primary arm's 228-compound concordance ρ. Expectation: same sign of ρ, wider CI at N=14.

## Role in cellduet

This is **not a primary arm**. The primary arm is Tahoe × `cpg0016-jump` (228 compounds, U2OS, B3b plan). The robustness arm is Tahoe × `rxrx3-core` (145 compounds, HUVEC, Phenom embeddings). The CPJUMP1 A549 arm is the **same-cell-line sanity check**.

**Statistical interpretation at N=14.** Limited. A 14-point Spearman ρ has a 95 % CI that spans roughly ±0.5 around the point estimate; "is the correlation positive at all" is the strongest claim the arm can support. P-values at N=14 are not meaningfully informative.

**Qualitative value.** Substantial. The single biggest caveat in the cpg0016 primary arm is the cell-line-context mismatch: 47 cancer lines (Tahoe) vs U2OS (osteosarcoma; cpg0016). A reasonable reviewer asks "your concordance might be entirely driven by the cell-line-context mismatch washing out cell-specific signal; what happens when the cell line is the same?" The CPJUMP1 sanity-check answers exactly that question, at small N, in the literally-same cell line (A549 Tahoe vs A549 CPJUMP1). If the 14-compound concordance ρ has the **same sign** as the 228-compound primary ρ and a comparable magnitude, the cell-line-context concern is empirically defused. If the signs disagree, the primary result is in trouble.

**Deliverable.** A single panel in the v0 figure: scatter of the 14 (transcriptomic distance, morphological distance) pairs from the same A549 cell line, with the 228-compound cpg0016 cross-cell-line scatter shown alongside for reference. One-paragraph caption framing it as a same-cell-line direction-of-effect check, not a separate significance result.
